# 02 UX Strategy

## Primary Users
- Health-conscious adults
- Fitness enthusiasts
- Busy professionals
- Parents
- Students

## User Personas

### Persona 1: Maya, 34, Health-Conscious Professional
- Values wellness and quality ingredients
- Buys premium food for daily routines
- Wants fast, simple ordering
- Frustrated by cluttered or overly salesy ecommerce sites

### Persona 2: Daniel, 29, Fitness Enthusiast
- Wants high-protein, nutritious products
- Needs clear nutrition information
- Looks for convenient options that support his routine

### Persona 3: Sara, 41, Parent
- Seeks family-friendly, wholesome food
- Wants trust, value, and practical ordering
- Appreciates easy reordering and bundles

## User Journeys

### Journey 1: Discover and Buy
1. Sees the homepage hero
2. Explores featured products
3. Reads benefits and nutrition
4. Adds product to cart
5. Completes checkout quickly

### Journey 2: Reorder and Repeat Purchase
1. Logs in or checks out as guest
2. Reorders favorite products
3. Adds bundle or family pack
4. Confirms delivery or pickup

### Journey 3: Browse for Inspiration
1. Visits recipes or snack ideas
2. Finds products to pair with ideas
3. Adds products to cart

## UX Goals
- Make the buying experience effortless
- Reduce friction at every decision point
- Increase trust through clarity and quality
- Encourage repeat purchases through convenience

## Conversion Points
- Hero CTA
- Featured product cards
- Product page add-to-cart
- Cart and checkout flow
- Bundle and subscription prompts

## Opportunities
- Clear nutrition-led storytelling
- Strong product photography
- Flexible pickup and delivery options
- Personalized recommendations
- Favourites and repeat order flow

# 04 Product Catalogue

## Catalogue Structure
- Greek Yogurt
- Greek Yogurt Slushies
- Healthy Snack Bowls
- Fruit Toppings
- Nuts
- Seeds
- Granola
- Bundle Packs
- Family Packs
- Limited Editions

## Product Content Requirements
Each product should include:
- Professional photography
- Description
- Ingredients
- Nutrition facts
- Calories
- Protein
- Carbs
- Fat
- Sugar
- Probiotics
- Available sizes
- Price
- Customer reviews
- Recommended pairings
- Related products

## Suggested Product Types
- Classic Greek Yogurt
- Mixed Berry Greek Yogurt Slushie
- Mango Flavor Yogurt
- Vanilla Bean Yogurt
- Honey Almond Yogurt
- Snack Bowl with granola and fruit
- Toppings such as berries, seeds, nuts, dates

## Catalogue UX Notes
- Use strong product cards with visual hierarchy
- Show key nutrition facts at a glance
- Include badges for popular, new, high-protein, or limited edition

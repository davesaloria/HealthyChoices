# 13 Launch Checklist

## Pre-Launch
- Brand messaging approved
- Product content complete
- Visual assets curated
- SEO metadata prepared
- Payment flow tested
- Mobile UX reviewed

## Launch
- Deploy to Vercel
- Verify Supabase connections
- Confirm analytics and tracking
- Test order flow end-to-end
- Validate accessibility

## Post-Launch
- Monitor conversions and user feedback
- Publish content updates
- Refine bundles and promotions
- Improve reviews and social proof

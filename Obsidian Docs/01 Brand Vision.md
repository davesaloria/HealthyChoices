# 01 Brand Vision

## Brand Personality
- Fresh and nourishing
- Premium but welcoming
- Honest and homemade
- Energetic and healthy
- Calm, clean, and trustworthy

## Target Audience
- Health-conscious adults
- Fitness enthusiasts
- Busy professionals
- Parents buying family-friendly snacks
- Students seeking convenient healthy options

## Customer Pain Points
- Confusion around healthy foods and nutrition
- Generic products that feel mass-produced
- Lack of trust in ingredient quality
- Difficulty finding convenient healthy options
- Overly complicated or overly commercial shopping experiences

## Customer Goals
- Eat healthier without sacrificing taste
- Find convenient, high-quality food options
- Build trust in a brand that feels authentic
- Enjoy wholesome products for themselves and their families
- Make quick, confident purchasing decisions

## Unique Selling Proposition
Healthy Choices blends homemade-style quality, Greek yogurt craftsmanship, and a premium wellness experience with approachable everyday convenience.

## Emotional Experience
The website should feel like a calm, uplifting wellness space that makes healthy living feel enjoyable, simple, and rewarding.

## Brand Voice
- Warm
- Clear
- Friendly
- Confident
- Honest
- Slightly playful, never preachy

## Visual Personality
- Minimal and contemporary
- Clean and editorial
- Fresh and inviting
- Premium without being sterile

## Colour Psychology
- Soft green: freshness, balance, health
- Cream and off-white: warmth, purity, calm
- Deep forest or charcoal: grounding and trust
- Accent coral or citrus: energy and appetite appeal

## Typography Direction
- Modern sans-serif with a friendly human touch
- High readability for product information and nutrition content
- Elegant but approachable hierarchy

## Photography Style
- Bright natural lighting
- Real ingredients and handmade textures
- Lifestyle imagery showing healthy eating moments
- Close-ups of yogurt, fruit, toppings, and bowls

## Illustration Style
- Minimal line art or soft abstract shapes
- Used sparingly for nutrition, wellness, and recipe storytelling

## Motion Style
- Smooth, elegant transitions
- Micro-interactions for cart, filters, hover states, and section reveals
- Never overly flashy

## Brand Vision Statement
Healthy Choices should feel like the premium, trustworthy home of wholesome yogurt and wellness-driven food experiences—where healthy eating feels beautiful, simple, and joyful.
